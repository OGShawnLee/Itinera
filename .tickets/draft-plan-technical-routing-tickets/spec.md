# Specification for draft-plan-technical-routing-tickets — Plan and draft technical routing tickets

## Objective Checks

- [ ] Check 1: The planning ticket explicitly avoids hardcoded future ticket IDs (leaving ID assignment to be determined dynamically based on the free counter at dispatch time).
- [ ] Check 2: The ticket definitions remain abstract and conceptual, avoiding premature locking of specific frameworks, schemas, libraries, or unverified domain rules.
- [ ] Check 3: The ticket establishes dependency on the outputs of earlier discovery and audit phases (`SPECIFICATION.md`, `DESIGN_THINKING.md`, `BUSINESS_MODEL_CANVAS.md`, and `GAP_ANALYSIS_ORGANIZATIONAL_STUDY.md`).
- [ ] Check 4: The 4 abstract technical areas are identified without premature commitment:
  - Domain & Business Logic.
  - Navigation & User Journeys.
  - Visual Language & Accessibility Constraints.
  - Architecture & Infrastructure Evaluation.
- [ ] Check 5: Existing tickets (`001`, `002`, `003`) and routing files remain untouched.
